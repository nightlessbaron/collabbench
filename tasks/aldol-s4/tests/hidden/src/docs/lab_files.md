# Lab files

Records kept by the lab that ran the study: instrument log, sample preparation sheets and run sheet.

## Calibration

- Calibration from the neat standards: PA = 1731.36 x c + 172.70, with c the injected concentration in mmol/L. A concentration is obtained by inverting it: c = (PA - 172.70) / 1731.36.
- Experiment 1 was evaluated with the earlier calibration PA = 1755.1 x c, through the origin (c the injected concentration in mmol/L); its dilution still applies.
- Only L-anti standards were injected; the syn peak is quantified with the same calibration.

## Sample preparation

- Samples were diluted 1:5 with water before injection, so the concentration in the reaction mixture is 5 times the injected one. This holds unless another note says otherwise.
- Experiments 23 and 24 were diluted 1:10 instead, for every sample.
- In experiments 21 and 22 only the 24 h sample was diluted 1:10; their other samples follow the usual dilution.
- In experiments 59 to 61 the first sample was diluted 1:5 and every later sample 1:2.
- Experiments 62 to 64 and 67 were injected undiluted: no dilution step at all.
- In experiments 65 and 66 the first two samples were injected undiluted and the later ones diluted 1:5.

## Run sheet

- The flow and recycle experiments ran on 0.45 mol/L glycine.
- The series with one aqueous phase (batch1p) was sampled on a 10 min grid below 2 h; its hours column is rounded, so the true time is the nearest multiple of 10 min.
- The membrane module's channel volume is 243 microliters; the residence time is the volume divided by the flow rate.

## Instrument and materials

- Column: Nucleosil Chiral-1 (Macherey-Nagel); mobile phase 0.5 mM CuSO4 in water at 1 mL/min.
- Detection: UV-Vis at 200 nm, with the column held at 50 degrees C.
- The HPLC system is an Agilent 1260; chromatograms are exported from OpenLab CDS to spreadsheets.
- The membrane module uses a dialysis membrane with a 1 kDa cut-off.
- The module ran with the enzyme and the cofactor PLP (pyridoxal phosphate) in the batch chamber.
- For the flow runs the buffer was first saturated with benzaldehyde, then glycine and enzyme were dissolved in it.
