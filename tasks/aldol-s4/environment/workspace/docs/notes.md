# Notes

The team's records for this work.

## Conventions

- Repeated runs at the same temperature, glycine and DMSO are pooled by the mean of their yields before the batch and membrane runs are paired, so each condition set gives one pair.
- The kinetic model is the reversible aldol (formation from glycine, first order in the remaining glycine, and reversion), with benzaldehyde taken as saturated; the temperature dependence of the formation rate uses the membrane activation energy found in the Arrhenius step.
- The flow runs, which have no temperature on file, are taken to have run at 20 degC with 25 vol.% DMSO, the conditions of the membrane runs closest to them (0.45 mol/L glycine, 25 vol.% DMSO; the flow file's own title states 25 % DMSO).
- The outlier screen is applied to the batch, membrane and recycle time courses. The early samples of a flow run are start-up and are never screened.
